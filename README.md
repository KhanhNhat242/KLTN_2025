# Bus Ticket Management System

## 1. Introduction

The passenger transport industry in Vietnam faces many challenges in ticket management, especially in bus services. Traditional ticketing systems are often manual, error-prone, and susceptible to congestion during peak hours and revenue leakage.

In the context of digital transformation, there is an increasing need for a smart ticketing system that integrates online payment and real-time trip tracking.

This project was developed to explore how technology can optimize operational processes, improve customer experience, and provide a scalable solution to support future growth.

---

## 2. Basic Information

The system consists of two main platforms:

- **Admin Dashboard** – A desktop-based platform for administrators.
- **Customer Mobile App** – A mobile application for customers.

### Customer Mobile App

The mobile application allows customers to:

- Search for and view information about trips and buses.
- Search for destinations, departure points, and routes.
- Find and book trips across provinces and cities in Vietnam.
- Select available seats when booking a trip.
- Make online payments within a limited time after making a reservation.
- View ticket information after successful payment.

### Admin Dashboard

Administrators can log in to the desktop application to manage:

- Trips
- Buses
- Destinations
- Departure points
- Customers
- Promotions
- Bookings
- Payment history
